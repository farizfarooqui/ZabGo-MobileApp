## 📖 About The Project

**ZabGo** is a dedicated mobility solution built to address everyday transportation challenges faced by students. From long waiting times and high commuting costs to safety concerns, this application provides a secure, reliable, and cost-effective way to travel.

By creating a closed and verified ecosystem, the app ensures that students can share rides confidently, save money, and build a stronger campus community.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🔐 Secure Identity Verification:** To maintain a trusted environment, every user must upload their valid Student ID (Front & Back) during the registration process.
- **➕ Offer & Request Rides:** Seamlessly post a ride with car details, route, fare, and available seats, or browse available rides to find the perfect match for your daily commute.
- **✔️ Complete Control for Drivers:** Drivers receive booking requests and have the full authority to either accept or decline them based on their convenience.
- **💬 In-App Messaging:** A built-in chat system allows drivers and passengers to communicate securely, coordinate pickup points, and ensure smooth travel.
- **📍 Detailed Trip Information:** Access comprehensive details about the ride, including departure times, vehicle information, and estimated arrival times.
